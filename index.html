Missing Japanese Tourist Found Dead, Linked to Serial Killings

Body first treated as a local resident; DNA analysis reveals identity of traveler who left Japan a week earlier

Unexplained Deaths Spread Fear Among Residents

Since early this month, local time, a series of unexplained deaths has been confirmed in the area, raising anxiety among residents. Local police believe the incidents are connected and are continuing their investigation.

According to sources close to the case, neither the identity of a suspect nor any leads have been made public, and vigilance across the area has been stepped up.

Police are urging residents to report any suspicious persons or circumstances immediately.

A passport recovered near the scene. Investigators have not disclosed where it was found.

DNA Identifies Body as Kanagawa Native

A Japanese man who had traveled abroad went missing and was later found dead. Local police initially treated the body as that of a local resident.

However, DNA analysis identified the remains as Mr. Himeno, a native of Kanagawa Prefecture who had left Japan one week earlier.

Police now believe the case is linked to the serial killings in the vicinity and are pressing ahead with their investigation.
